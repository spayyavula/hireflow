## Execution plan

### Posting

- **Day/time:** same day as the LinkedIn post. ~9-10am PT, Tuesday or Wednesday. Twitter's algorithm is more time-of-day-agnostic than LinkedIn's, but the launch-momentum compounds if both go live the same day.
- **No hashtags.** Twitter actively suppresses hashtag-heavy posts in 2026. The thread + good copy is enough.
- **Don't tag** specific accounts in the post itself unless they've pre-agreed. Tagging without permission = algorithm penalty.
- **Pin Tweet 1** to your profile for ~7 days. New profile visitors see it first.

### First 60 minutes after posting

Even more critical on X than LinkedIn — the algorithm decides whether to amplify within ~30 minutes.

- **Reply to every reply within 15 minutes.** Substantive replies, not just "thanks!" — Twitter weighs reply *length* and depth as engagement quality.
- **Quote-tweet your own Tweet 1** with a follow-up specific (e.g. the H-1B 60-day rule) — this is a legitimate algorithm boost.
- **Don't ask for RTs in the post body.** Algorithm penalty. The natural CTA in Tweet 4 ("tell a friend who needs it") does the work.

### Day-1 amplification

- **DM your 10-15 closest dev-Twitter friends BEFORE posting publicly.** Same as LinkedIn — pre-seeded engagement in the first 30 minutes is what triggers algorithmic boost. Sample DM: *"Launching Hyrly on Twitter today around 9am, link incoming. If you have a sec to RT or reply in the first hour I'd really appreciate it. No worries if you're busy."*
- **Reply to recent layoff-tagged tweets** from people in your network with a single, useful sentence + the relevant link (severance calculator, H-1B landing page, etc.). Don't shill — be useful. Twitter rewards reply-network behavior.

### Day-2 to 7 amplification

- **Quote-tweet the original thread 48-72 hours later** with one new specific (a user reaction, a new datapoint, a comment thread highlight). New algorithmic surface, same content reach.
- **Reply to high-profile threads about layoffs/tech with the calculator link.** Examples: Pragmatic Engineer / Lenny / Andrew Chen / GergelyOrosz threads. Substantive contribution first, link second — never the other way around.
- **DM 5 dev-Twitter founders who'd be interested** with a specific ask: *"Built Hyrly for laid-off engineers. Wouldn't pitch you to RT — but if you have someone in your network who just got laid off, the triage at hyrly.ai may help them. Free, no signup."* This is genuinely useful + low ask + soft credit-claim if they reshare.

### What success looks like

- **48 hours:** 50+ likes on Tweet 1, 10+ RTs, 5+ replies from people you don't know, ~30 triage starts attributable via UTM tag
- **7 days:** 1-2 mid-account reshares (>5k followers), first cold reply from someone saying "tried this, it actually helped"
- **30 days:** the original thread surfaces in search for "h1b laid off" / "severance calculator" / "tech layoff resources" reply chains

### What failure looks like

- 5-10 likes, all from your closest follows, zero RTs from beyond your immediate network. → The hook didn't land. Try the single-tweet version next week, or wait 30 days and try again with a specific data point ("X users have run the severance calculator, average result was Y").

## Cross-promotion with the LinkedIn launch

Post LinkedIn first (~8-9am PT), then X (~9-10am PT, after the LinkedIn launch is live so you can link to it). On the LinkedIn post, drop the X thread URL in a pinned comment. On the X thread Tweet 4, you don't need to link to LinkedIn — most X users don't follow back to LinkedIn anyway.

If you have a personal Substack/newsletter, send the launch email 24 hours later as the "I just shipped this — here's the whole story" long-form version. Don't duplicate-post; the email is the "director's commentary" pass.

---

## Things to NOT do

- **Don't** quote-tweet competitors negatively in the launch thread. (Implicit contrast in the post body is fine; explicit callouts make enemies.)
- **Don't** post the same thread to LinkedIn — the formats are different audiences with different attention spans. Use the LinkedIn version for LinkedIn and the X thread here.
- **Don't** delete & re-post if it underperforms in the first hour. Twitter's algorithm pattern-matches that as low-quality behavior.
- **Don't** add UTM tags to the hyrly.ai link in Tweet 4. Twitter sometimes flags tracking parameters and suppresses reach. Use server-side referrer tracking instead.

## Open editorial decisions

1. **"30 friends over two years"** — same question as the LinkedIn post. Real number? If vaguer is more truthful, *"a lot of friends"* still works. The specific count just adds credibility.
2. **Tweet 4 phrasing** — *"Tell a friend who needs it"* is the soft amplification ask. If that feels too sales-y, swap with *"Hope it helps if you're in this."* (more vulnerable, lower-pressure).
3. **Quote-tweet the LinkedIn post?** You could quote-tweet the LinkedIn URL from Tweet 4 instead of the soft amplification line. Higher cross-traffic, but feels more "marketing." Your call.
4. **Add a tweet about being open to feedback?** Optional Tweet 5: *"What's missing from this for you? Reply or DM. I'll add it."* Good for engagement, slightly dilutes the launch CTA. Skip if you're not actively iterating.
